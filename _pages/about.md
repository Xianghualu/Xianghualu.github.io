I am a Professor at the School of Management, Fudan University, and a member of the [Department of  Information Management and Business Intelligence](https://www.fdsm.fudan.edu.cn/department/xxgl/zhuanye.aspx). 
My research explores how emerging digital technologies shape human behavior and create value for individuals, organizations, and society. My current research focuses on Human-AI Collaboration, user behavior in the digital economy, and the value and management of new technologies. My work has been published in leading journals in information systems, management, and marketing, including *MIS Quarterly*, *Information Systems Research*, and *Journal of Marketing*.
